### 2026 Teachers’ Day Update: Special Visit Arrangements for Serangoon StaRs Alumni
### 

Dear Serangoon StaRs Alumni,

As we approach Teachers’ Day, we would like to share an important update regarding visits to the school.

Due to the tight schedule for our Teachers’ Day Celebration on 3 September, we regret that we will not be able to accommodate visits from ex-students on the day itself.
We are heartened to know that some of you would like to return to school to express your appreciation and gratitude to your former teachers. You are welcome to make an appointment with your teachers during Term 4 Week 1 instead. This will allow you to have a more meaningful interaction with your teachers without the constraints of the Teachers’ Day programme.

For those visiting the school, please take note of the following:

<br>•	Come dressed appropriately and in accordance with the school’s dress code and expectations of behaviour.

<br>•	Report to the Guardhouse upon arrival and follow the instructions given by our security personnel.

<br>•	Once granted access to the school, please wait at the Foyer or General Office for the teacher whom you have made an appointment with.

<br>•	For security reasons, please do not enter other areas of the school premises without permission.

We sincerely appreciate your understanding and cooperation. We look forward to welcoming you back to Serangoon Secondary School and hope that your visit will be a meaningful opportunity to reconnect with your former teachers.

Thank you.
